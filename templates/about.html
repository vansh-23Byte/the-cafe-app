A small roastery and bakehouse.

We opened in 2019 with a second-hand roaster, one oven and a stubborn idea that a neighbourhood cafe should make its own things. That has not changed much. The green coffee comes from three importers we visit, the flour from a mill two hours north, and the plums, when they are good, from a stall at the Sunday market.

Everything on the menu is made here except the butter and the milk. If you ask what is in something, whoever is on the bar will actually know.

Roasting

Tuesdays and Fridays, 6am. The shop smells extraordinary and gets a little smoky. Bags of last week's roast are half price on Monday.

The bake

Doughs are mixed the night before and shaped at five. When a tray is gone it is gone, which is why the croissants disappear by ten.
